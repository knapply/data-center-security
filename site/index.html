      About

      This tracker covers the data center industry end to end. On the security side: physical threats (fire, power, cooling, sabotage, cable cuts), IT/OT/SCADA vulnerabilities in facility and server infrastructure, and supply-chain risk. On the industry side: energy deals and grid limits, new capacity and construction, market moves, and regulation.

      Market data comes from TradingView widgets and may be delayed. It is shown for context, not as investment advice.

      Every entry is curated: drafted from public sources, checked against an editorial rubric, and reviewed before publishing. Locations are shown at city or region level only. Where no source names the maker of a failed part, any best guess is labeled as one, with its evidence.

      Data and methodology live in the GitHub repository.
